Article 1

What Replere is

Replere offers recorded sophrology sessions, to be done after physical exertion and at moments that call for one. The app is published by Anatole Pitron, in a non-professional capacity (see the legal notice).

Article 2

What Replere is not

Replere is not a medical device and does not replace a doctor, a physiotherapist, a psychologist or a sophrologist. The sessions are a wellness aid. They do not diagnose anything and do not treat anything.

In the event of pain, feeling unwell, psychological distress or any doubt about your state of health, stop the session and consult a healthcare professional.

Never do a session while driving or swimming, or in any situation that requires your attention.

Article 3

Your account

The account is local to your device. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential. You may delete your account at any time from Settings, Profile.

Article 4

Free access and Replere Plus

The following are free, without limit and without advertising: all sessions, the daily path with its phrase of the day, the Basics (Les bases) program, the music of the week, the streak and the map of sophrologists. The sessions will always remain free.

Replere Plus is an optional subscription, monthly or annual, which adds: the memory and reflection exercises, the other programs, the argumentation coach, your data in detail (including the recovery curve) and the history of your sessions. It is paid through your Apple account, renews automatically and can be canceled at any time from that account's settings, no later than 24 hours before the end of the current period. The music of the week cannot be purchased: it is composed as you practice.

What is free today will remain free. If a paid plan is offered in the future, it will bring new features, and its terms (price, duration, renewal, cancellation) will be presented before any purchase and added here.

Article 5

The map of sophrologists

The professionals displayed come from public sources or third-party mapping services. The publisher does not select them, does not recommend them and has no commercial relationship with them. Their presence does not constitute an endorsement of their competence: since sophrology is not a regulated profession in France, check their qualifications before making an appointment.
