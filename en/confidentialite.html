Privacy Policy

This English translation is provided for convenience. The French version is the legally binding reference.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Summary

In short

Replere runs on your iPhone. Your account, your sessions, how you felt and your health data stay there: they are not sent to the publisher. There is a single exception, if you connect Garmin: Garmin only sends your workouts to a server, so a small Replere server receives them and passes them on to your iPhone (section 4). Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence. Only a few pieces of information leave the phone, each for a specific feature that you turn on: they are listed below, together with their recipient.

1. Data controller

Anatole Pitron, publisher of Replere, who can be reached at contact@replere.app. No data protection officer has been appointed: this is not mandatory for this processing.

4. If you connect Garmin Connect

Garmin transmits the data from its watches only to a server, never directly to an app. If you connect your Garmin account in Replere (Settings → Connected apps), here is exactly what happens.

  • What Garmin sends: the summary of your workouts (sport, start and end time, duration, distance, average and maximum heart rate) and the daily summary of your health (resting heart rate, sleep, stress). Nothing else: not your location, not your routes, not your menstrual cycle data.
  • Why: to suggest the right session as soon as a workout ends, and to show you how your recovery is evolving. No other use: no advertising, no profiling, no resale, no training of artificial intelligence.
  • Where: a Replere server, hosted by Scaleway in France (European Union). It receives the data, encrypts it, and delivers it to your iPhone the next time your iPhone connects. No one else receives it: no audience measurement, advertising or artificial intelligence tool has access to it.
  • For how long: the data is erased from the server as soon as your iPhone has received it, and at the latest after 30 days. The Garmin access token, which is encrypted, is kept for as long as Garmin remains connected.
  • Legal basis: your explicit consent, given on Garmin's authorization screen. This data includes health data (Article 9 of the GDPR).
  • To stop: disconnect Garmin in Replere, or remove access in Garmin Connect (Settings → Third-Party Apps). The token and all Garmin data still on the server are then deleted. To obtain a copy or a correction of this data, write to contact@replere.app.

5. When you write to us

An email to contact@replere.app or to sophrologues@replere.app is routed by Cloudflare and then received in a Gmail (Google) mailbox, two companies established in the United States. We keep your address and your message there in order to reply to you, on the basis of our legitimate interest in handling your request, for 3 years after our last exchange, after which we delete them.

6. The replere.app website

The website sets no cookies, loads no external fonts, scripts or other external resources, and does not measure its audience: no consent banner is therefore necessary. Its host, GitHub (United States), technically records visitors' IP addresses for the security of the service, in accordance with its own policy.

7. Retention periods

  • App data: on your iPhone, for as long as you keep your account. It disappears when you delete your account or the app.
  • Strava, Suunto and Garmin tokens: until you disconnect the service.
  • Garmin data on the server: erased as soon as your iPhone has received it, at the latest after 30 days.
  • Emails: 3 years after the last exchange.

8. Your rights

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection, the right to withdraw your consent at any time, and the right to set out instructions regarding what happens to your data after your death.

  • In the app, without having to ask anyone: Settings → Profile → “Export my data” (portability) or “Delete my account” (immediate and permanent erasure).
  • Withdrawing a permission: iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security (Health, Location, Calendars, Bluetooth), or Settings → Connected apps in Replere.
  • For everything else: write to contact@replere.app. We respond within one month.

If you believe that your rights are not being respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority): cnil.fr/fr/plaintes.

9. What Replere does not do

  • No automated decision-making producing legal effects, no advertising profiling.
  • No access to your contacts, your microphone or your photo library (only the photo you choose is passed on by iOS).
  • No sale or rental of data.

10. Minimum age

Replere is intended for people aged 15 and over. Below that age, a parent's consent is required for the features that rely on consent (health, location, calendar).

11. Changes

Any change to this policy is dated at the top of the page. If it changes the way your data is used, the app will inform you beforehand.
