In one sentence

Replere offers a few minutes of sophrology right after the effort, to help the body wind down, with your heart measured if you wear a watch.

In one paragraph

Replere is an iPhone sophrology app designed for after sport. Instead of a morning meditation, it offers short voice-guided sessions at the moment the body winds down: after training, before a big event, before sleep. With an Apple Watch, a Garmin watch or a heart rate strap, the app shows your heart rate during the session. The catalog is free and ad-free, and your data stays on your iPhone.
