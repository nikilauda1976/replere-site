Frequently asked questions

Replere FAQ

Short answers to the questions we hear most often.

The questions

What is Replere?

Replere is an iPhone sophrology app for recovery after sport. It offers short voice-guided sessions to do right after the effort, to help the body wind down: slow breathing, muscle relaxation, visualization. It is published in France by Anatole Pitron.

Which sophrology app should I use to recover after sport?

Replere is designed for exactly that: a few minutes right after your workout, as your body winds down, with your heart rate shown if you wear a watch. It also offers sessions for sleep, pre-race nerves and waking up.

Is Replere free?

The essentials are: every session (forever), the daily path, the Basics program, one exercise for the mind per day with its feedback, the music of the week, your streak and the map of sophrologists, all with no ads. Replere Plus, an optional subscription, adds the other programs, unlimited exercises, the argument coach, your data in detail and your session history.

Does it work with a Garmin or an Apple Watch?

Yes. With an Apple Watch, a Garmin watch (by broadcasting heart rate) or a Bluetooth heart rate strap, Replere shows your heart during the session and how far it comes down. Without a watch, sessions work just the same.

How is it different from a meditation app like Headspace or Petit BamBou?

Those are general-purpose meditation apps. Replere specializes in the time after effort: it shows up as your body winds down, can spot your workout in Apple Health or Strava to suggest a session, and measures your heart during the session.

How long is a session?

From a minute and a half to a few minutes. The shortest post-workout session lasts ninety seconds, standing, eyes open.

What about Android?

Not yet. For now, Replere is available on iPhone and iPad only.

Does my health data go anywhere?

No. Your account, your sessions and your health data stay on your iPhone. One exception, if you connect Garmin: your workouts pass, encrypted, through a Replere server in France, which deletes them as soon as your iPhone has received them. Replere shows no ads and uses no trackers.

Is sophrology proven?

Many of its tools are: slow breathing speeds up the return to rest after exercise, and muscle relaxation improves sleep in several trials. Sophrology as a discipline has been studied much less, with mixed results. Replere cites these studies and their limits.

Does Replere replace a sophrologist or a doctor?

No. Replere is a wellness companion, not a medical device. It does not replace a doctor, a physical therapist or a sophrologist.

How can I try Replere?

Replere is in free beta. Install TestFlight, Apple's testing app, then open the beta link on your iPhone. A question? Write to contact@replere.app.
