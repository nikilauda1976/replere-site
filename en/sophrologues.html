For sophrologists

Your practice, at the moment athletes need it.

Replere offers short sophrology sessions right after the effort. We are looking for sophrologists who would like their sessions featured for runners, trail runners and athletes.

Submit my session

What we offer

Your session, featured

In the app's Guidance section, with your name, your certification, your city and a link to your website.

Motivated athletes

People who train and are already looking to recover better, sleep better or handle the stress of a big event.

No fees

Nothing to pay, either way. No exclusivity: you stay free to do as you wish.

How it works

Step 1

You write to us

Send your name, your certification, your city, your website and your session idea (intention, length) to sophrologues@replere.app.

Step 2

We talk it over

Together, we check that the session suits athletes and the app's short format.

Step 3

Your session goes live

It appears in the app with the next update. You can remove it at any time.

What you can expect, honestly

Replere is a young app, in beta. We don't promise you a number of clients. We offer you the chance to be among the first practitioners visible in an app designed for athletes, and to grow with it.

The app's map of sophrologists, separate from this feature, comes from the French public business directory: it is not a recommendation.
