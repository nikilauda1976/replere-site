Guide · Sleep

Run in the evening and still sleep well?

It's one of the most widespread myths. The research mostly says the opposite, with one important exception.

Updated October 12, 2026 · 4 min read

What the meta-analysis shows

A meta-analysis published in 2019 in Sports Medicine pooled 23 studies in healthy adults. The conclusion: evening exercise doesn't harm sleep, and even slightly increases the share of deep sleep. Read the study

The exception: an intense effort that ends less than an hour before bedtime can delay falling asleep and shorten sleep time. The authors advise scheduling hard sessions and competitions earlier when possible.

Sleeping well after an evening session

  • Save intervals for earlier in the day; in the evening, favor easy endurance runs.
  • Leave at least an hour between the end of a hard session and bedtime.
  • Help your body come down: a lukewarm shower, dim lights, a few minutes of slow breathing.
  • Relax your body one area at a time once you're in bed. Progressive muscle relaxation improved sleep quality in a meta-analysis of 31 trials. Read the study

These studies involve healthy adults. If you have chronic insomnia, talk to a doctor.
